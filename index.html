Our Story

One recipe. Three generations. No changes.

It started in a small kitchen with a steel tin, a wooden spoon, and a grandmother who refused to measure anything. The recipe was never written down — it was taught by feel, by the sound the dough makes when it’s ready.

We still bake in small batches. We still grind cardamom by hand. And we still refuse to add anything that would let a nan khatai sit on a shelf for six months, because it was never meant to.

If it doesn’t crumble when you pick it up, it isn’t nan khatai. — the only rule we were given
