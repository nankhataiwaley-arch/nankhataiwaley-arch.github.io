Our Story

Every recipe has a story

It began in a small home kitchen with a steel tin, a wooden spoon and a recipe that was never written down — only taught, by feel, from one generation to the next.

What started as something we baked for family and neighbours slowly became something people began asking for by name. We still bake in small batches, we still grind our own cardamom, and we still refuse to add anything that would let a Nan Khatai sit on a shelf for six months.

If it doesn't crumble when you pick it up, it isn't Nan Khatai. — the only rule we were given
